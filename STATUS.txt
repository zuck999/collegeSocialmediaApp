=================================================
ENCRYPTION SPECS
=================================================

Algorithm: AES-128-RSA Hybrid

Symmetric (AES-128):
- Block size: 128 bits
- Key size: 128 bits
- Rounds: 10
- Padding: PKCS7

Asymmetric (RSA):
- Key size: 512 bits (demo)
- Public exponent: 65537
- Prime test: Miller-Rabin

Performance:
- Encrypt: 50-100ms
- Decrypt: 100-200ms
- Total: ~300ms (imperceptible)
